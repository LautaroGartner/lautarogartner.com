The exact query was checked in Google with English language, a US country preference and personalization disabled. This is still a browser-session sample, not a guaranteed US-local ranking report. The app and article were not observed on the first results page. Results included Calculator.net, Edmunds, Tidemark Federal Credit Union, California Bank & Trust, Bank of America, Autotrader, Kelley Blue Book, Navy Federal and U.S. News. No claim is made that a particular feature caused their rankings.

Search Console separately confirmed the app and both guide languages indexed. The latest page-filtered performance data had no impressions and was six hours behind the launch. A meaningful average position is unavailable, not zero.

## Changes prepared and published

- Exact, descriptive app title and visible topic label: Car Loan Calculator with Tax and Fees. The approved brand heading and shimmer remain.
- A one-click illustrative tax/fee scenario, alongside the original no-tax example.
- Live result itemization: tax dollars, fees and price before financing, followed by loan payments and full purchase cost.
- Public HTML table comparing no tax/fees, financed tax/fees and upfront payment. All computed rows use the tested loan module; the worked explanation also states inputs and rounding.
- Specific answers about title, registration and documentation fees, avoiding double-counting an out-the-door quote, and tax assumptions. No unverified state-specific tax rates.
- Guide title, opening answer and screenshot focus on the same buyer task. Explanatory sections support the tool rather than duplicating an entire calculator on a second URL.
- Clearer internal links from the homepage, project, article and app. Reciprocal language links and structured-data relationships remain intact.

The core original example remains $35,000 price, $5,000 down, 8% fixed annual interest and 60 months, without tax or fees. The separate tax example uses 7.5% and $450 in fees. It estimates $670.64 per month when financed; paying these costs upfront keeps the regular loan payment at $608.29 and requires $3,075 extra upfront. Financing them adds $666.01 interest under this model. These are illustrative calculations, not quotes.

## Measurement

Once fresh data is available, compare query-level impressions, clicks and average position for the exact phrase and close variants, split by page, country and device. Review after a full week of data, then again after 28 days; those intervals are measurement windows, not ranking promises. Check whether the calculator or guide receives the matching query and improve the useful page based on that evidence. Actual changes in rankings and AI citations must be observed, not inferred from metadata deployment.
